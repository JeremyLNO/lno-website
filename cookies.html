Cookie Policy.

What this website stores in your browser, and which third-party services it loads.

Last updated — July 2026

This site sets no cookies

This website (lno.company) does not set any cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising or marketing tags, no tracking pixels, and no third-party measurement scripts. Nothing on this site follows you across other websites, and no consent banner is shown because there is nothing to consent to.

What is stored in your browser

One item is written to your browser's local storage, and only when you use the site:

  • lno-lang — the interface language you pick with the EN / FR / ES / DE switch, so the site opens in that language on your next visit. It contains only a two-letter language code. It stays on your device, is never transmitted to us, and is not used to identify you.

You can remove it at any time by clearing site data for lno.company in your browser settings. The site continues to work normally without it, and simply opens in its default language.

No third-party services are loaded

Every asset on these pages — stylesheets, scripts, images, and typefaces — is served from lno.company itself. The typefaces (IBM Plex Serif, IBM Plex Mono, and Archivo) are hosted on our own servers rather than loaded from Google Fonts, so viewing this site makes no request to Google or to any other outside party, and no third party receives your IP address.

Scope of this page

This page describes the public lno.company website only. The LNO Control Center dashboard and its companion iOS app are separate, access-restricted tools; how they handle data is described in our Privacy Policy.

Changes to this page

If we add a service that sets cookies or otherwise changes what is stored in your browser, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent beforehand. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
