Privacy Policy.

How LNO collects, uses, and protects data across the LNO Control Center dashboard and mobile app.

Last updated July 2026

Who this applies to

This policy covers the LNO Control Center web dashboard (cc.lno.company) and its companion iOS app. Both are private, internal tools used by LNO staff and, on a limited read-only basis, by LNO shareholders. They are not available to the general public. Access requires an account created by an LNO administrator.

What we collect

We collect the minimum data needed to operate the dashboard securely:

  • Account identity: your name and email address, obtained either via "Sign in with Google" (restricted to @lno.company accounts) or, for external shareholders, an emailed one-time sign-in code.
  • Portfolio data: the trading positions, fund allocations, equity history, and alerts your account is permitted to view. This data belongs to LNO and is not personal data about you.
  • Security logs: sign-in timestamps, IP address, and device/browser information, used to detect suspicious activity and lock accounts after repeated failed sign-ins.
  • Push notification token: if you enable alerts on the iOS app, a device token is registered with our notification provider so we can deliver them. You can disable this at any time in Settings.
  • Preferences: display settings such as language and saved filters, stored locally on your device.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing. We do not track you across other apps or websites. The iOS app does not collect data beyond what is described here, and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Third-party services we rely on

To operate the dashboard, limited data passes through the following processors, strictly for the purposes described:

  • Google: identity verification for @lno.company sign-in ("Sign in with Google").
  • OneSignal: delivery of push notifications, if you opt in.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (hosting, database) store account and portfolio data securely; they do not use it for their own purposes.
  • Exchange market-data APIs (e.g. Binance) provide public price data only; no personal data is shared with them.

Data security

Passwords and one-time codes are never stored in plain text. Sensitive configuration is encrypted at rest. Access to the dashboard requires authentication, and administrative actions are logged for accountability.

Data retention

Account and portfolio data is retained for as long as your account is active. Security logs are retained for a limited period for fraud-prevention purposes. When an account is removed by an administrator, associated personal data is deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the address below. Because accounts are provisioned and managed by LNO administrators, some requests may be fulfilled by your administrator directly.

Children's privacy

This app and dashboard are internal business tools not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes to the dashboard or applicable law. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to contact@lno.company.
